// OUR STORY

NaRo Studios was born from a shared obsession — two friends, Romano and Nathan, who spent more nights than they can count playing games and dreaming about making their own.

We believe games are the most powerful storytelling medium alive. Every mechanic is a metaphor, every world a mirror. We build experiences that stay with you long after the screen goes dark.

From concept to code, from art direction to audio — we pour ourselves into every detail. Small team. Big vision. No shortcuts.

Romano
CO-FOUNDER · CREATIVE DIRECTOR

Visionary designer and world-builder. Romano shapes the look, feel, and soul of every NaRo game — from concept art to narrative architecture.

Nathan
CO-FOUNDER · LEAD DEVELOPER

Engineering the impossible, one system at a time. Nathan turns ambitious ideas into performant, polished gameplay experiences players love.
